<summary>Maintainers: how to add a supporter</summary>

3. Mirror the entry in `src/shared/supporters.json` (app) and `src/supporters.json` in the landing repository: `{ "name": "…", "tier": "sponsor", "url": "https://…" }`.
4. For Coffee, thank them in the next release notes.
5. Sponsors get the `supporter` label on their issues; collect roadmap votes in a pinned Discussion.
